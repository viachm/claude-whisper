Claude Whisper vs Wispr Flow

Wispr Flow is a polished commercial dictation service for desktop and phone, with paid plans and team features. Claude Whisper is a free, open-source desktop app that hands your speech to Claude Code’s own voice dictation. Here is where each one fits.

Quick verdict

Free on top of Claude Code, or a full dictation service

If you already use Claude Code on a Mac or Windows PC and want hold-to-talk dictation in every app without another subscription, Claude Whisper does the job. If you want dictation on your phone as well, richer rewriting (styles, snippets, Command Mode) or team plans with compliance paperwork, Wispr Flow offers much more, for a monthly price once you pass the free allowance.

Pick Claude Whisper if

  • You already use Claude Code and don’t want another subscription.
  • You want the source code: it is MIT licensed and on GitHub.
  • You use Windows on ARM, which Wispr Flow does not support.
  • You prefer an app whose only connections of its own are update checks on GitHub, and that keeps its history on your computer.

Pick Wispr Flow if

  • You want dictation on iPhone or Android as well as the desktop.
  • You don’t use Claude Code or don’t want a Claude account.
  • You want per-app styles, snippets, a shared dictionary or editing by voice.
  • Your company needs SSO, admin controls, SOC 2 / ISO 27001 or a HIPAA BAA.

Price

What each one costs

Claude Whisper

The app is free and MIT licensed. There are no plans or word allowances of its own: dictation runs on the Claude account you are already signed in to Claude Code with, and Improve uses your Claude subscription. The catch is that it is only “free” on top of a Claude account that can use Claude Code.

Wispr Flow (as of November 2026)

  • Free: “Limited words per week”. Wispr’s homepage FAQ says 2,000 words a week; its help center says the allowance differs by platform and account. At the cap, new dictations are blocked until the reset.
  • Pro: $15 per user a month, or $12 billed annually. Unlimited dictation.
  • Growth: $23 per user a month, or $18 annually; $33 / $26 with unlimited Notetaker.
  • Enterprise: custom pricing, billed annually.
Privacy

Where your voice goes

Claude Whisper

The app does no speech recognition of its own. A hidden Claude Code session, signed in as you, transcribes your speech, so the audio goes to Anthropic under your own account. Apart from update checks on GitHub when you ask, the app itself makes no network connections; nothing is sent to Claude as a chat message, and the last 30 dictations stay on your computer. Improve (macOS) sends the transcribed text, not audio, to a separate claude -p process.

Wispr Flow

Audio is processed in Wispr’s cloud. Two switches, “Improve the model for everyone” and Dictation cloud storage, are on by default; cloud storage keeps transcripts, audio and history on Wispr’s servers. Context Awareness, also on by default, reads the text around your cursor and on-screen text (Wispr says it takes no screenshots). Wispr says your data is never sold, it holds zero-data-retention agreements with its AI providers, and only Enterprise gets “Models never trained on your data”.

Platforms

Desktop only, or desktop and phone

Wispr Flow covers more ground: Mac (macOS 12 or later), Windows 10/11 on x64, iPhone and Android. It does not run on Windows on ARM, iPad, Linux, virtual machines or remote desktops.

Claude Whisper is desktop only: macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon or Intel, and Windows 10/11 on x64 or ARM64. The Windows app is a first version without Improve. There is no phone app.

Features

What you get beyond plain dictation

Claude Whisper

  • Hold-to-talk on Right Command (Right Ctrl on Windows), a hands-free lock, Esc to cancel.
  • Improve on macOS: tap Shift while talking and Claude removes fillers and self-corrections before pasting.
  • History of the last 30 dictations; “Paste and press Return” to send straight away.
  • Pastes followed by a space and puts your clipboard back.

Wispr Flow

  • Writing styles (Formal, Casual, very casual, Excited) per app category, English only.
  • Command Mode on paid plans: edit selected text, draft a reply or search by voice.
  • Dictionary, voice snippets and team sharing; a Notetaker on Mac and Windows.
  • 100+ languages with auto-detect.

Claude Whisper’s limits, plainly

  • It needs Claude Code installed and signed in to a Claude account, with /voice working. If you do not use Claude Code, it is not for you.
  • Your speech goes to Anthropic through Claude Code, under your account. It is not an offline or on-device tool.
  • Desktop only: macOS 14 or later and Windows 10/11. No iPhone or Android app.
  • The Windows app is a first version: no Improve, no capsule position and no Faster start yet.
  • The builds are not notarized or signed, so macOS and Windows warn on the first launch, and macOS asks for its permissions again after each update.
  • It is unofficial: not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
Fair is fair

When Wispr Flow is the better choice

  • You want to dictate on your iPhone or Android phone, not only on the desktop.
  • You don’t use Claude Code, or you would rather not tie dictation to a Claude account.
  • You want more control over the output: per-app styles, snippets, a learning dictionary and Command Mode.
  • Your team needs SSO/SAML, admin control over model training, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 or a signed HIPAA BAA.
  • You want a commercial product with its own help center rather than an unsigned open-source app.
FAQ

Questions about Claude Whisper and Wispr Flow

Is Claude Whisper a free alternative to Wispr Flow?

For desktop dictation, yes: the app is free and open source, with no plans of its own. It needs Claude Code installed and signed in to a Claude account, and it has fewer features than Wispr Flow (no phone apps, styles, snippets or team plans).

Do either of them work offline?

No. Wispr Flow needs an internet connection for every dictation, and Claude Whisper relies on Claude Code’s voice dictation, which sends your speech to Anthropic.

Can I run both on the same Mac?

Their default keys differ (Fn for Wispr Flow, Right Command for Claude Whisper), so they can sit side by side. If you move one of them to the other’s key, quit or rebind the other.

Which one cleans up my text better?

Wispr Flow has more cleanup options: styles, a dictionary, snippets and Command Mode. Claude Whisper’s Improve is a single step on macOS that removes fillers and self-corrections and fixes punctuation and term spelling, keeping your words and language.

Is Claude Whisper made by Anthropic?

No. It is an unofficial, independent project that works with Claude Code. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
