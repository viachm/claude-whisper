Claude Whisper vs other dictation apps

Claude Whisper is a free, open-source app that works with Claude Code: hold a key, speak, and the text is pasted at your cursor. It is not the right tool for everyone. Here is how it stacks up against the paid apps and the dictation built into macOS and Windows, with each one’s real strengths.

Who it is for

Where Claude Whisper fits, and where it doesn’t

A good fit if

  • You already use Claude Code and want dictation in every app without another subscription.
  • You want hold-to-talk on one key, on macOS or Windows.
  • You want the source code: it is MIT licensed and on GitHub.

Look elsewhere if

  • You don’t use Claude Code or don’t want a Claude account.
  • You need offline or on-device transcription.
  • You want dictation on your phone.
  • You need team plans, SSO or compliance such as SOC 2 or HIPAA.

Try Claude Whisper

Free and open source. If Claude Code already works on your computer, setup takes a couple of minutes.
