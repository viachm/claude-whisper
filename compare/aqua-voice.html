Claude Whisper vs Aqua Voice

Aqua Voice is a commercial AI dictation app with its own speech models, screen-aware spelling and apps for desktop and phone. Claude Whisper is a free, open-source desktop app that hands your speech to Claude Code’s own voice dictation.

Download for macOS Download for Windows

Written by the developer of Claude Whisper. Facts about Aqua Voice come from its own pages, read in November 2026; every one is listed under Sources.

Quick verdict

Feature-rich and screen-aware, or free and minimal

Aqua Voice does more: it reads your screen to spell names and code right, lets you edit selected text by voice, shows words in real time on its Max plan, and runs on phones. Claude Whisper is the free, minimal choice if you already use Claude Code on your computer.

Pick Claude Whisper if

  • You already use Claude Code and want no extra subscription.
  • You don’t want an app that reads your screen.
  • You want the source code: it is MIT licensed.
  • Desktop dictation is all you need.

Pick Aqua Voice if

  • You want names and code spelled from what is on your screen.
  • You want to edit selected text by voice, or words that appear as you speak.
  • You want iPhone and Android apps.
  • You need SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA or SSO.

Price

What each one costs

Claude Whisper

Free and MIT licensed, with no tiers or word allowances of its own. It runs on the Claude account you are already signed in to Claude Code with; Improve uses your Claude subscription.

Aqua Voice (as of November 2026)

  • Free: 1,000 words to start, which Aqua’s FAQ treats as a one-time allowance. No credit card.
  • Pro: $12 a month, or $10 billed annually; unlimited words, custom instructions, dictionary.
  • Max: $24 a month, or $19 billed annually; Realtime Mode, voice commands, HIPAA BAA.
  • Business: $24 per seat a month, or $228 per seat a year, minimum 2 seats. Students get 70% off Pro and Max.
Privacy

Where your voice goes

Claude Whisper

Claude Code transcribes your speech under your Claude account, so the audio goes to Anthropic. Apart from update checks on GitHub when you ask, the app itself makes no network connections; it does not read your screen and keeps the last 30 dictations on your computer.

Aqua Voice

With Privacy Mode off, Aqua stores dictation audio and transcripts on its servers to provide and improve the service; with it on, it does not (session metadata may still be collected). Deep Context reads your screen. Aqua shares data with advertising partners for ad measurement, and says it never shares transcripts or audio with them. Local history keeps recent audio on your device for 3 days.

Platforms

Where each one runs

Aqua Voice runs on Mac (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10/11, iOS 26 or later and Android 10 or later. It also offers an MCP server, a CLI and a speech API.

Claude Whisper runs on macOS 14 or later and Windows 10/11 (x64 and ARM64); the Windows app is a first version without Improve. There are no phone apps.

Features

What you get beyond plain dictation

Claude Whisper

  • Hold-to-talk on Right Command (Right Ctrl on Windows), hands-free lock, Esc to cancel.
  • Improve on macOS: removes fillers and self-corrections, fixes punctuation and term spelling.
  • History of the last 30 dictations; “Paste and press Return” to send straight away.

Aqua Voice

  • Deep Context: spelling of names and code from what is on screen.
  • Edit Mode: select text, hold the key, say the change.
  • Realtime Mode (Max): words appear as you speak.
  • Custom dictionary, writing style and custom instructions.

Claude Whisper’s limits, plainly

  • It needs Claude Code installed and signed in to a Claude account, with /voice working. If you do not use Claude Code, it is not for you.
  • Your speech goes to Anthropic through Claude Code, under your account. It is not an offline or on-device tool.
  • Desktop only: macOS 14 or later and Windows 10/11. No iPhone or Android app.
  • The Windows app is a first version: no Improve, no capsule position and no Faster start yet.
  • The Windows build is not signed yet, so SmartScreen warns on the first launch.
  • It is unofficial: not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
Fair is fair

When Aqua Voice is the better choice

  • You want dictation that uses your screen to spell names, terms and code correctly.
  • You want to rewrite selected text by voice, or see words appear as you speak.
  • You want iPhone and Android apps.
  • You need compliance (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA BAA) or team billing with SSO and SCIM.
  • You want developer hooks such as an MCP server, a CLI or a speech API.
  • You don’t use Claude Code.
FAQ

Questions about Claude Whisper and Aqua Voice

Is Claude Whisper a free alternative to Aqua Voice?

For basic desktop dictation, yes. Aqua’s free tier is 1,000 words to start; Claude Whisper has no allowance of its own but needs Claude Code and a Claude account, and it lacks Aqua’s screen context, Edit Mode and phone apps.

Does Claude Whisper read my screen?

No. It holds Claude Code’s voice key in a hidden session and pastes the result. Aqua’s Deep Context reads the screen you are on.

Can I run both?

Aqua uses Fn by default on Mac and Claude Whisper uses Right Command, so they can coexist as long as they are on different keys.

Which one supports more languages?

Aqua lists 49. Claude Whisper understands the languages Claude Code’s voice dictation supports; you pick one in Settings.

Is Claude Whisper made by Anthropic?

No. It is an unofficial, independent project that works with Claude Code. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

Try Claude Whisper

Free and open source. If Claude Code already works on your computer, setup takes a couple of minutes.
