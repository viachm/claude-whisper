Claude Whisper vs Apple Dictation

Every Mac already has Dictation: press a key, talk, and macOS types. Claude Whisper is a free, open-source menu bar app that adds hold-to-talk on Right Command and hands your speech to Claude Code’s voice dictation instead. Here is when the built-in one is enough.

Quick verdict

Built in and on-device, or hold-to-talk with Claude cleanup

For most people, Apple Dictation is the place to start: it is already there, many languages run on your Mac, and there is nothing to install or sign in to. Claude Whisper suits people who already use Claude Code and want press-and-hold dictation, a hands-free lock, a history of recent dictations, and optional cleanup of fillers and self-corrections.

Pick Claude Whisper if

  • You want push-to-talk: hold a key, speak, let go.
  • You want fillers and self-corrections taken out (Improve).
  • You want a history of recent dictations to copy again.
  • You already use Claude Code.

Pick Apple Dictation if

  • You want nothing to install and no extra account.
  • You want speech processed on your Mac, in a supported language.
  • You also dictate on iPhone or iPad.
  • You want spoken punctuation and emoji commands.

Price

Both are free, with different strings

Claude Whisper

The app is free and MIT licensed. It runs on the Claude account you already use for Claude Code, and Improve uses your Claude subscription. You need Claude Code installed and signed in first.

Apple Dictation

Included with macOS at no extra cost. No separate sign-up beyond your Mac.

Privacy

On your Mac, at Apple, or through Claude Code

Claude Whisper

Claude Code transcribes your speech under your Claude account, so the audio goes to Anthropic. Apart from update checks on GitHub when you ask, the app itself makes no network connections, and it keeps the last 30 dictations on your computer.

Apple Dictation

For languages processed on device, what you say is not sent to Apple; Keyboard settings show which these are. Otherwise speech is sent to and processed by Apple. Apple says audio is not stored unless you opt in to Improve Siri and Dictation. Request data is tied to a rotating random identifier, not your Apple Account, and may be kept for up to two years.

Platforms

Where each one runs

Apple Dictation is part of macOS, and Dictation also exists on iPhone and iPad. On Apple silicon you can keep typing while you dictate, and switch languages mid-dictation.

Claude Whisper runs on macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel, and on Windows 10/11 as a first version. It needs two permissions on the Mac: Accessibility, to see the key and paste, and Microphone.

Features

Toggle and type, or hold and paste

Claude Whisper

  • Hold Right Command, speak, let go: the text is pasted at your cursor. Double-tap or Right Option to lock hands-free.
  • Improve: tap Shift while talking and Claude removes fillers and self-corrections, fixes punctuation and spelling of terms.
  • History of the last 30 dictations; “Paste and press Return” to send straight away.

Apple Dictation

  • Press the Microphone key or your shortcut to start and stop; dictate any length without a timeout.
  • Automatic punctuation, spoken punctuation and emoji commands.
  • Blue-underlined alternatives for words it was unsure of.
  • On macOS 27 with Apple Intelligence: better spelling and punctuation in English on M3 or later.

Claude Whisper’s limits, plainly

  • It needs Claude Code installed and signed in to a Claude account, with /voice working. If you do not use Claude Code, it is not for you.
  • Your speech goes to Anthropic through Claude Code, under your account. It is not an offline or on-device tool.
  • Desktop only: macOS 14 or later and Windows 10/11. No iPhone or Android app.
  • The Windows app is a first version: no Improve, no capsule position and no Faster start yet.
  • The Windows build is not signed yet, so SmartScreen warns on the first launch.
  • It is unofficial: not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
Fair is fair

When Apple Dictation is the better choice

  • You want dictation with nothing to install and no extra account.
  • You want your speech processed on your Mac: many languages run on device.
  • You dictate in a language Apple supports but are not sure Claude Code’s dictation does.
  • You also dictate on iPhone or iPad and want the same behaviour there.
  • You like spoken commands for punctuation and emoji, or picking alternatives for unclear words.
  • You don’t use Claude Code.
FAQ

Questions about Claude Whisper and Apple Dictation

Why use Claude Whisper if my Mac already has Dictation?

Mainly for press-and-hold on one key, a hands-free lock, a history of recent dictations, and Improve, which removes fillers and self-corrections. If you don’t need those, Apple Dictation is simpler.

Does Apple Dictation work offline?

In languages processed on device, yes. Apple’s Keyboard settings show whether you need an internet connection. Claude Whisper always needs one.

Can I keep using Apple Dictation alongside Claude Whisper?

Yes. They use different keys: Apple’s Microphone key or Dictation shortcut, and Right Command for Claude Whisper.

Which is more private?

Apple Dictation in an on-device language keeps your speech on your Mac. Claude Whisper sends it to Anthropic through Claude Code, under your account.

Is Claude Whisper made by Anthropic?

No. It is an unofficial, independent project that works with Claude Code. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
